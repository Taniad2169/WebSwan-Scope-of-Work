WebSwan Scope of Work for Web Design Services

1. Project Overview
We begin with a free demo at no cost and with no obligation. This demo requires approximately 15 minutes of your time to review the design and discuss any updates or changes you would like. If you decide not to proceed, you pay nothing. Once the presentation is complete and the website's core structure and design links are in place, we will collect full payment and continue working to complete the remaining links and content.

2. Deliverables
Custom website design and layout based on client's branding and requirements. Development of responsive website pages optimized for desktop and mobile devices. Integration of essential features such as contact forms, galleries, and social media links, security measures and set up of website hosting.

3. Pricing and Payment Terms
Please refer to our pricing page. Our pricing includes everything discussed in the scope of work.

4. Client Responsibilities
Provide clear direction, feedback, and content for the website. Review and approve design mockups and website drafts in a timely manner. Collaborate with the designer during the development process.

5. Revisions and Change Requests
Two rounds of revisions are included in the design fee. For additional editing after the two rounds the cost is $75/revision/edits.

6. Testing and Quality Assurance
Thorough testing of the website across different browsers and devices to ensure compatibility and functionality.

7. Launch and Handover
Finalization of website design and functionality. Transfer of website ownership and necessary credentials to the client. Client is responsible to link their domain with a third party.

8. Legal Terms
Limitations of liability and warranty disclaimer. Ownership of intellectual property rights upon full payment of design fee.

9. Refund and Payment Disclaimer
The demo is provided free of charge, with no obligation to pay. If you approve the demo and wish to proceed, a 50% down payment is required to start the project. The remaining 50% balance is due once the website work is completed, tested, and fully functional. No refunds will be issued after all website links are in place and the website is tested. Any changes requested after completion will be charged at $75 per edit.

Website Design Service Agreement
Once your website is fully designed, tested, and running smoothly, it becomes your responsibility to connect your domain. We do not collect or sell your information to third parties. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.
